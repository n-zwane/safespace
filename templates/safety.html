Creating a Safety Plan

A safety plan can help you prepare for potentially dangerous situations. Here's how to create one:

Identify Safe Spaces

Determine safe places you can go in an emergency - friends' houses, family members' homes, or public places like police stations or hospitals.

Prepare an Emergency Bag

Keep a bag with essential items ready: important documents, medication, some clothes, money, and emergency contacts.

Establish a Code Word

Create a code word with trusted friends or family that lets them know you need help without alerting anyone else.

Plan Your Exit Strategy

Identify the safest ways to leave your home or current location in different scenarios (when alone, with children, etc.).

Save Emergency Contacts

Program emergency numbers into your phone under disguised names and keep a written copy in your emergency bag.

Immediate Danger

If you are in immediate danger:

  • Try to get to a safe location if possible
  • Call 10111 for police assistance
  • Use the Quick Exit button if someone approaches
  • If you can't talk, dial 112 and leave the line open
