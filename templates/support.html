Frequently Asked Questions

Is the support confidential?

Yes, all support services are completely confidential. Your information will not be shared with anyone without your explicit consent, except in situations where we are legally required to report (such as imminent risk of harm to yourself or others).

How much does counselling cost?

We offer a sliding scale fee structure based on your financial situation. Many of our services are free, and we can connect you with additional resources if needed. No one is turned away due to inability to pay.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

We strive to offer crisis support within 24 hours and regular counselling appointments within a week. Our chatbot and peer support are available immediately.

Can I choose a counsellor of a specific gender?

Yes, we understand that gender preferences are important in the healing process. You can specify your preference when booking, and we will match you with an appropriate counsellor.

What if I need help outside of business hours?

Our chatbot is available 24/7, and we have an emergency hotline for crisis situations after hours. You can also schedule appointments for evenings and weekends with some of our counsellors.
